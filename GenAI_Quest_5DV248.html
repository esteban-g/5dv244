From tokens to trustworthy use

You have seen the basic chain:

context→ representation→ probability→ sampling→ generated text
Generation ≠ retrieval
A fluent continuation is not automatically a verified fact.
Sampling matters
Different decoding settings can produce different outputs.
Attention ≠ explanation
Internal attention patterns are not a complete account of why an output is correct.
Human evaluation remains essential
For consequential use, inspect evidence, provenance, uncertainty and limitations.

🎓 Final Agent Academy Briefing

You have now moved from individual intelligent agents to systems containing multiple agents.

In a multi-agent system, agents operate within a shared environment and may interact with one another.

Their relationships may involve:

🤝 Cooperation
Agents coordinate their actions to achieve compatible or shared objectives.

⚔️ Competition
Agents may have conflicting objectives or compete for limited resources.

📡 Communication
Agents can exchange information that influences their decisions.

🤝 Negotiation
Agents may attempt to reach agreements when their interests are not identical.

The key idea is that once several intelligent agents inhabit the same environment, understanding the behavior of one agent is no longer sufficient. We must also consider the interactions among agents.

🧭 Investigation 2: Four perspectives

Your investigation has uncovered an important problem: there are different ways of thinking about what an intelligent machine should do.

The lecture organizes four foundations of AI around two questions:

  • Are we concerned with thought or behavior?
  • Should the machine behave humanly or rationally?

🧠 Thinking humanly

The cognitive modeling approach asks whether a computer is thinking in ways similar to humans.

💬 Acting humanly

The Turing Test approach asks whether a computer can behave in a way that is indistinguishable from a human in the relevant interaction.

📐 Thinking rationally

The "laws of thought" approach is concerned with codifying correct reasoning.

🎯 Acting rationally

The rational-agent approach focuses on agents acting to achieve the best outcome, or the best expected outcome under uncertainty.

🔎 Investigation: What changed?

Look at the timeline you just reconstructed.

Notice that AI history is not simply a story of one technology becoming increasingly powerful. Different ideas and approaches have appeared, disappeared, and re-emerged.

One important lesson

The history of AI includes different approaches to creating intelligent systems.

Some approaches focus on representing knowledge and reasoning, while others focus on learning from data.

Your lecture later summarizes these as knowledge-oriented mechanisms ("solvers") and machine-learning mechanisms ("learners"). :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

🧠 Think about this:

If an AI system can perform a task very well, does that necessarily mean that it thinks like a human?

Or could it be using a completely different mechanism to achieve the same result?

🧠 Key takeaway

AI has a long and diverse history. The field has developed through different ideas about computation, reasoning, learning, language and intelligent behaviour.

The timeline in the lecture is deliberately simplified and biased. It does not represent the complete history of AI.

In particular, your lecture points out that important figures such as Charles Babbage and Ada Lovelace are missing, and that the timeline does not adequately represent the more recent development of Human-Computer Interaction and Human-AI Interaction. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Mission 2: Fit the Model

You are now entering the Regression Lab. Your mission is to discover how a machine learning model learns a relationship between an input and a numerical output. You will control the parameters of a linear model yourself.

🧩 Step 1 — Meet the model

In simple linear regression, we use a model of the form:

y = β₀ + β₁x

β₀ is the intercept.
β₁ is the slope.
x is the input variable.
y is the predicted output.

Your task: The data points are already given. Your job is to find values of β₀ and β₁ that make the line fit the data well.

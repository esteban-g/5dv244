Challenge 1 — Fact or conclusion?

Consider the following ASP program:

bird(eddy).
penguin(eddy).

flies(X) :- bird(X), not penguin(X).
Which of the following is an explicitly stated fact?
A. bird(tux)
B. flies(tux)
C. flies(eddy)
D. not penguin(tux)

Challenge 3 — What about Eddy?

Eddy is known to be both a bird and a penguin. The rule is:

Should flies(eddy) be derived?
A. No. The condition not penguin(eddy) is not satisfied.
B. Yes. Every bird must fly.
C. Yes. Penguins are birds.

Key idea to remember:

An ASP program describes a problem using facts and rules.

Facts represent information that is explicitly given. Rules describe conditions under which new information can be derived.

The resulting collection of facts and derived conclusions forms an answer set.

Notice also that the rule

flies(X) :- bird(X), not penguin(X).

does not say that birds fly. It says that flies(X) can be derived when X is a bird and X is not known to be a penguin.

The Autonomous Agent

Your final reasoning challenge

You have learned how to represent knowledge, derive conclusions, handle constraints, and construct plans.

Now it is time to put everything together.

You are the AI engineer responsible for an autonomous robot operating in a hospital.

The robot must reason about its environment, choose appropriate actions, and achieve its goal without violating the constraints of the environment.

Can you design the reasoning process?

🧠 Stage 1 — What Does the Robot Know?

The robot receives the following knowledge:

patient(alice).
medicine(aspirin).

location(alice,room1).
location(aspirin,pharmacy).

robot_at(robot,pharmacy).

The robot needs to deliver the medicine to Alice.

What information is explicitly represented?

🔎 Stage 2 — Derive New Knowledge

The robot also has the following rule:

ready_to_deliver(X) :-     medicine(X),     location(X,pharmacy),     robot_at(robot,pharmacy).

Given the facts from the previous stage, what can the robot conclude?

🛡️ Stage 3 — Respect the Constraints

The hospital contains a restricted area.

restricted(room3).

:- robot_at(robot,room3).

What does this constraint express?

🗺️ Stage 4 — Construct the Plan

The robot is currently at the pharmacy.

The medicine must reach Alice in room 1.

The available actions are:

move(pharmacy,room1).
move(pharmacy,room2).
move(room1,pharmacy).
move(room2,room1).

Which sequence achieves the goal?

⚔️ Final Challenge — Design the Agent

🚨 Emergency Delivery

The hospital robot must deliver medicine from the pharmacy to a patient in room 1.

However:

  • The robot cannot enter restricted areas.
  • The robot must start at the pharmacy.
  • The medicine must reach room 1.
  • The plan must consist only of valid actions.

A simplified ASP representation is:

robot_at(robot,pharmacy).
medicine_at(aspirin,pharmacy).
patient_at(alice,room1).
restricted(room3).

move(pharmacy,room1).
move(pharmacy,room2).
move(room2,room1).
move(room1,pharmacy).

% Goal
:- not medicine_at(aspirin,room1).

% Safety constraint
:- robot_at(robot,room3).
What makes this an intelligent-agent problem?

🚨 Emergency Delivery

The hospital robot must deliver medicine from the pharmacy to a patient in room 1.

However:

  • The robot cannot enter restricted areas.
  • The robot must start at the pharmacy.
  • The medicine must reach room 1.
  • The plan must consist only of valid actions.

A simplified ASP representation is:

robot_at(robot,pharmacy).
medicine_at(aspirin,pharmacy).
patient_at(alice,room1).
restricted(room3).

move(pharmacy,room1).
move(pharmacy,room2).
move(room2,room1).
move(room1,pharmacy).

% Goal
:- not medicine_at(aspirin,room1).

% Safety constraint
:- robot_at(robot,room3).
What makes this an intelligent-agent problem?

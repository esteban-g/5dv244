THE BAYES INVESTIGATION

Mission 2: Updating beliefs with evidence

In the previous mission, you learned that uncertainty can be represented using probabilities.

But intelligent agents rarely receive all the information they need at once.

Instead, they observe evidence and must update their beliefs.

Your mission is to investigate how evidence changes probability.

🔬 Investigation 1 — Before the evidence

Imagine an intelligent diagnostic agent trying to determine whether a patient has a particular condition.

Before receiving any additional information, the agent has a prior probability.

What does a prior probability represent?

🧠 Investigation 2 — Evidence arrives

Now the agent receives evidence.

We are no longer asking:

P(Cavity)

Instead, we ask:

P(Cavity | Evidence)

This is a conditional probability. It represents our belief after taking the evidence into account.

What happens when new evidence is incorporated?

⚖️ Investigation 3 — The Bayes Rule

Bayes' rule provides a formal relationship between a hypothesis, evidence, and our updated belief.

P(H | E) =

P(E | H) × P(H)
─────────────
P(E)

The important idea is not simply memorizing the equation.

Think of it as a mechanism for moving from:

Prior belief + Evidence → Updated belief

Which quantity represents the updated belief?
